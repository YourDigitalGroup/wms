Why the Material Choice Matters for Rochester Projects

Fulton County's climate means your masonry has to perform. Winters in the Rochester area bring freeze-thaw cycles that stress absorptive materials, and summer humidity can accelerate efflorescence on improperly sealed surfaces. Selecting brick and stone rated for the regional climate — and pairing them with the right mortar type — is the single biggest factor in long-term durability.

For exterior applications in Indiana's severe-weathering zone, look for brick that meets ASTM C216 Grade SW standards. SW-grade brick is manufactured and tested to withstand repeated freeze-thaw exposure without spalling or surface degradation — an important baseline for any Rochester project exposed to the elements.
