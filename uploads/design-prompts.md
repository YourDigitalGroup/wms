# Design Prompts

## Numbers & Typography
- When using numbers (like steps) don't put a 0 in front of a number. Example: 4, not 04.

## Navigation
- Place phone number with icon as the last nav item.
- Have nav turn to burger on mobile and tablet (with phone icon to the left of nav burger).
- Don't use HOME in nav.

## Responsive Breakpoints
- Use these consistent responsive breakpoints for all elements: 1024px wide, 880px wide, 767px wide.
- Optimize site design for tablet and mobile views.

## Cards
- Don't use rounded side-tabbed borders on any cards.

## Eyebrows
- Don't use line elements or pulsating dots in eyebrows.
- Don't place eyebrow text in a box or shape.

## Contact Form
- On a one-page site, consolidate the contact form at the bottom with the footer.

## Testimonials
- If testimonials are needed, use a carousel showing 3 on desktop, 2 on tablet, and 1 on mobile.

## Gallery
- If a gallery is needed, make images clickable for a navigable slideshow.
- Images don't need text on them.
- If many images are in the gallery, show a LOAD MORE button after 10 or 12 images instead of loading all images.

## Footer
- Very bottom of footer, left side: copyright and company name and Privacy Policy.
- Right side: "Powered by Fourge" (linking out to [fourge.com](http://fourge.com)) and "Site by 44i" with link to [44i.com](http://44i.com). (This "site by" info will change per site.)

## Images & SVGs
- Do not stretch, recolor, or redraw SVGs unless prompted to do so.
- Optimize images for web.
- When using a photo of a person, don't cut off a head.

## Social Icons
- When using social icons, always use the logo icons — don't use text-only logos.

## Phone Numbers
- Always make phone numbers clickable call links.
