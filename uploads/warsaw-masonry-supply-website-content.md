# GLOBAL SEO AND AEO RECOMMENDATIONS

These apply across the whole site and are what separate a page that reads well from a page that ranks and gets cited by AI answer engines.

## Structured Data (Schema)
- Add **LocalBusiness** schema sitewide (in the footer or header) with the exact NAP, hours, geo coordinates, and service area. This is the single most important technical step for local visibility.
- Add **FAQPage** schema to every FAQ block on the site. This makes your answers eligible for rich results and highly citable by AI answer engines.
- Add **Service** schema to each service on the Services page.
- Add **BreadcrumbList** schema to support navigation and search display.

## AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
- The FAQ answers on this site are written so the first sentence directly answers the question. Keep that pattern for any future content, because AI answer engines extract those opening sentences.
- Allow AI crawlers in `robots.txt` (for example GPTBot, PerplexityBot, and Google-Extended). Blocking them means zero visibility in AI-generated answers.
- Build local authority content over time. Queries like "best masonry supply in Warsaw IN" and "where to buy pavers near me" are exactly where a local family business can win.

## On-Page and Technical
- Use one H1 per page (provided above), with a logical H2 and H3 structure beneath it.
- Write descriptive image alt text using natural language, for example "natural stone patio built with Warsaw Masonry Supply pavers in Warsaw Indiana." Name image files descriptively, not "IMG_1234.jpg."
- Internal linking: the Home page should link to Products, Outdoor Living, and Services. Services should link to related Products. Every page should link to the Contact page. This spreads authority and guides visitors toward the primary conversion action.
- Ensure the site is fast and mobile-friendly. Most local searches happen on phones, and page speed affects both search ranking and AI crawler access.
- Confirm the Google Business Profile is claimed, complete, and uses the exact same NAP shown on the site.

## Conversion
- Keep a visible "Schedule Your Appointment" or "Request a Free Quote" call to action in the header on every page and repeat it at the bottom of each page. It is your primary conversion action and should never be more than one scroll away.
- Once social profiles are created, add them to the footer and to your LocalBusiness schema to strengthen entity signals.
